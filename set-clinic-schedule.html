Clinic Schedule Configuration

Set hours per weekday, multiple breaks, custom appointment lengths, and public holidays. Booking is blocked during breaks and on holidays.

Hours By Day

Enable each day the clinic is open, and set that day's open and close times.

Breaks (Lunch And Other)

Add as many breaks as you need. Appointment slots cannot be booked during these times.

Appointment Lengths




Custom Lengths

Add lengths such as 35 minutes. They become selectable for the default slot length and visit types.

Public Holidays

Name and mark closed days for the year. Appointments cannot be scheduled on these dates.
